Welcome to the Grand Food & Culture Festival

Experience delicious food, traditional recipes and cultural performances from different regions of India. The Grand Food & Culture Festival brings together people, food lovers, artists and chefs to celebrate India's rich cultural diversity. Visitors can enjoy delicious dishes, live cooking shows, cultural performances and music while spending quality time with family and friends.
