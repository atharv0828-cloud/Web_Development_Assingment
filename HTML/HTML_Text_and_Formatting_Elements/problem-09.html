Student Terms and Rules

General Rules

1. Students must carry their valid college identity card whenever they are on the college campus. The identity card may be required for entry into classrooms, laboratories, libraries, and other college facilities.

2. Students are expected to attend classes regularly and arrive on time. Students should maintain a responsible attitude towards their academic schedule and avoid unnecessary absence from classes.

Attendance

1. Students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in each subject. Students with attendance below the required percentage may not be permitted to appear for the final examination. Attendance shortage may result in academic penalties.

Students who are absent due to genuine reasons should inform the concerned faculty member and provide the required documentation whenever applicable.

Examination

1. Students must carry their college identity card and examination admit card to every examination. Students must follow all instructions given by the invigilator during the examination.

2. Any form of cheating, copying, or use of unauthorised material during an examination is strictly prohibited. Students found using unfair means may face disciplinary action and cancellation of their examination.

Assignment Submission

1. All assignments must be submitted before the deadline specified by the respective faculty member. Assignments should be properly completed and submitted in the required format.
Example deadline: All assignments must be submitted by 11:59 PM on the announced submission date.

Students are advised not to wait until the last moment to submit their work, as technical problems or internet issues may cause unnecessary delays.

Discipline

1. Students must maintain discipline and behave respectfully with faculty members, staff, and fellow students. Any form of bullying, harassment, abusive behaviour, or deliberate damage to college property will not be tolerated.

2. Mobile phones and other electronic devices must be used responsibly inside classrooms. Students should keep their devices on silent mode during lectures and examinations. Violation of classroom rules may result in disciplinary action.

Important Notice

Students are responsible for reading and following all academic and institutional rules. These rules are intended to create a safe, respectful, and productive learning environment for everyone.
