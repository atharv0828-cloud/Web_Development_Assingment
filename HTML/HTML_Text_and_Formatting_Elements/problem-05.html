Chemistry

Chemistry is the study of matter and its interactions.

water: H2O

carbon dioxide: CO2

oxygen: O2

sulphuric acid: H2SO4

Physics

Physics is the branch of science that deals with matter, energy, motion, and their interactions.

Area is measured in square metres: m2

Volume is measured in cubic metres: m3

Speed is measured in metres per second: m/sEinstein's equation: E = mc2

Einstein's equation: E = mc2

Gravitational acceleration: 9.8 m/s2

Biology

Biology is the study of living organisms and their structure, functions, growth, and reproduction.

The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life.

Photosynthesis helps green plants convert light energy into chemical energy.

The human heart pumps blood throughout the body and supplies oxygen and nutrients to different organs.
