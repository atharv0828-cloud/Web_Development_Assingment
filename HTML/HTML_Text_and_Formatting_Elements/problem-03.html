City Launches Major Green Initiative

New campaign aims to make the city cleaner and more environmentally friendly

The city administration has launched a major environmental campaign to encourage citizens to keep their surroundings clean and reduce pollution. The initiative includes tree plantation drives, waste management programs, and awareness campaigns across different areas of the city.

According to officials, the campaign will begin on 15 August 2026 and will involve schools, colleges, local communities, and businesses. More than 10,000 trees are expected to be planted during the first phase. Authorities have also requested citizens to actively participate in the campaign.

The administration said that the initiative is not only focused on planting trees but also on developing long-term habits among residents. Every small effort can make a significant difference to the environment. Special cleanliness drives will also be conducted in public areas and residential neighborhoods.

Editor's Note

Environmental protection is a shared responsibility. Citizens can contribute by keeping their surroundings clean, reducing waste, and protecting green spaces. The success of this initiative will depend on the active participation of the community.

Local Schools Introduce New Technology Programs

Students to receive hands-on experience with modern digital tools

Several schools across the city have announced new technology-based programs designed to improve students' digital skills. The programs will include basic coding, robotics, artificial intelligence, and other practical activities.

The new initiative will officially begin on 1 September 2026. More than 5,000 students are expected to participate in the first phase of the program. Teachers will also receive special training to help students understand the new subjects effectively

School officials believe that technology education will help students prepare for future careers and develop better problem-solving abilities. The aim is to make learning more practical, interactive, and engaging.

Editor's Note

Both initiatives highlight the importance of community participation and education in building a better future. Students and citizens can play an important role by actively participating in such programs and contributing positively to society
