Employee Leave Request Form

Employees can use this form to submit a leave request. Please provide accurate employee information, select the appropriate leave type, enter the leave dates and provide a valid reason for the request.

Leave Categories

Leave Category Description
Casual Leave For personal or short-term requirements.
Sick Leave For illness or medical-related reasons.
Earned Leave Leave accumulated through employment.
Work From Home For working remotely instead of attending the office.
Other For leave requests that do not fit the above categories.

Note: Leave requests are subject to approval by the concerned manager or department.
