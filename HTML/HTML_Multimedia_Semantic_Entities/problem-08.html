Understanding Personal Finance

Personal finance involves managing your income, expenses, savings, borrowing and investments. A simple budget can help you understand where your money goes each month.

Always compare financial products carefully and understand their terms before making a decision.

Financial Education Articles

1. Why Should You Create a Budget?

A budget is a plan for how you will use your income. It can help separate needs from wants and encourage regular saving.

A useful approach is to record your income and divide your expenses into categories such as food, transport, education, entertainment and savings.

2. Building an Emergency Fund

An emergency fund is money kept aside for unexpected expenses. Saving a small amount regularly can gradually build a useful financial safety net.

"Small, consistent financial habits can make a meaningful difference over time."

3. Understanding Interest

Interest is the cost of borrowing money or the return earned on deposited or invested money.

For example, if the principal is ₹10,000 and the annual rate is 5%, simple annual interest would be ₹500.

Formula: Interest = Principal × Rate × Time

4. Understanding EMI

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment. It is a regular payment made towards certain loans.

EMI depends on factors such as loan amount, interest rate and repayment tenure.

Smart Financial Tips

  • Track your income and expenses.
  • Create a realistic monthly budget.
  • Save regularly whenever possible.
  • Compare interest rates before borrowing.
  • Read terms and conditions carefully.
  • Keep banking passwords confidential.
  • Avoid sharing OTPs with other people.
  • Review recurring expenses regularly.

Common Finance Terms

Interest Rate
The percentage used to calculate interest on money borrowed or deposited.
EMI
Equated Monthly Instalment paid towards certain loans.
Principal
The original amount of money borrowed or invested.
Tenure
The period for which a loan or financial arrangement continues.
