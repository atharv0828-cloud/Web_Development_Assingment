TechFest 2026 - Innovate. Create. Transform.

Introduction

TechFest 2026 is an exciting college technology festival designed to bring together students who are passionate about technology, innovation, programming, and creativity. The event will provide participants with an opportunity to showcase their technical knowledge, work on interesting challenges, and learn from other students with similar interests. The festival is designed to encourage students to move beyond classroom learning and explore practical applications of technology.

Students from different colleges and academic backgrounds can participate in various activities and competitions during the event. Whether you are interested in coding, artificial intelligence, robotics, web development, or emerging technologies, TechFest 2026 provides an environment where participants can experiment with ideas and turn them into practical solutions. TechFest 2026 will be one of the biggest student-focused technology events of the academic year.

Highlights

The festival will feature coding challenges, technical competitions, project demonstrations, innovation activities, workshops, and interactive sessions. Participants will be able to work individually or as teams and compete against students from different colleges. These activities are designed to test logical thinking, creativity, technical knowledge, teamwork, and problem-solving abilities.

One of the major attractions of the festival will be the project showcase, where participating teams can present their technology projects and innovative ideas. The event will also include sessions focused on modern technologies such as artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, cloud computing, and software development. The main objective is to encourage students to experiment, collaborate, and learn through practical experiences.

Date/Venue

TechFest 2026 will be conducted over two days and will include competitions, workshops, project demonstrations, and guest sessions. Participants are advised to reach the venue before the scheduled starting time so that registration and event formalities can be completed smoothly.

Date: 18 September 2026 - 19 September 2026
Venue: GLA University, Mathura Campus
Location: 17km Stone, NH-19, Delhi-Mathura Road
City: Mathura, Uttar Pradesh

Registration Deadline: 10 September 2026

Speakers

TechFest 2026 will feature guest speakers and technology professionals who will share their experiences, knowledge, and insights with participating students. The sessions will focus on current developments in technology, career opportunities, innovation, entrepreneurship, and the skills students can develop to prepare themselves for the modern workplace.

Participants will have the opportunity to listen to experienced professionals and ask questions during interactive sessions. These sessions are intended to give students practical perspectives about technology and professional careers. The final speaker lineup and session schedule will be announced through the official event information page.

Registration

Students interested in participating in TechFest 2026 can register online by completing the registration process before the deadline. Participants should provide accurate information while registering and carefully read the instructions related to their selected competitions and activities. Early registration is recommended because some events may have limited participation capacity.

Registrations close on 10 September 2026. Students are encouraged to complete their registration before the deadline to avoid last-minute technical or submission problems. Participants should also keep their registration details safely stored for future reference.
