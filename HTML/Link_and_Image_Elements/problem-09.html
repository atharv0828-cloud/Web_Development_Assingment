Brew & Bean Café

Fresh Coffee, Great Food, and Good Vibes

Welcome to Brew & Bean Café, a cozy fictional café located in the heart of Agra. Our café is designed as a comfortable place where students, families, friends, and professionals can relax, enjoy freshly prepared food, and spend quality time together. We believe that a great café is not only about food and drinks but also about creating a warm atmosphere where people feel comfortable and welcome.

About Us

Brew & Bean Café brings together freshly prepared beverages, delicious snacks, and a relaxed environment. Our menu is inspired by popular café favourites while also including a few creative combinations for customers who enjoy trying something different. Every order is prepared with attention to freshness, presentation, and flavour.

Whether you want to meet friends after college, work on your laptop, enjoy a quiet cup of coffee, or celebrate a small occasion, Brew & Bean is designed to provide a comfortable experience. Our signature coffee is prepared using freshly ground beans and is one of the café's most popular specialities.

Signature Items

Classic Brew Coffee is our signature beverage, prepared with freshly ground coffee beans and served hot. It has a rich aroma and balanced flavour, making it suitable for both regular coffee lovers and people who simply want a relaxing drink.

Our Belgian Chocolate Waffles are served warm with chocolate sauce and seasonal toppings. They are a popular choice for customers looking for a sweet snack to enjoy with coffee or another beverage. The combination of a crispy outside and soft centre makes them a favourite among dessert lovers.

We also serve freshly prepared grilled sandwiches with vegetables, cheese, and a selection of sauces. The sandwiches are suitable for customers looking for a filling snack or a light meal. Our menu is designed to offer different options so that customers can choose according to their preferences.
