Welcome to GLA University

GLA University is a private university located in Mathura, Uttar Pradesh, offering undergraduate, postgraduate, doctoral and diploma programs across multiple academic disciplines. The university provides students with opportunities to gain academic knowledge, practical experience and professional skills through classroom education, laboratory work, projects and extracurricular activities. The institution focuses on providing students with a combination of academic learning and practical exposure.

The university campus provides academic buildings, laboratories, library facilities, hostels, sports facilities and spaces for student activities. Students can participate in technical, cultural, sports and innovation-related activities during their academic journey.

About Institution

GLA University provides higher education in areas including engineering, computer applications, management, pharmacy, science, humanities, law, agriculture and other disciplines. The university's academic environment is supported by laboratories, classrooms, libraries and other learning facilities that allow students to develop theoretical understanding as well as practical skills.

The university also encourages students to participate in innovation, research, technical competitions, cultural activities and sports. A student's university experience can extend far beyond regular classroom teaching when they actively participate in projects and campus activities.

GLA University is situated at 17km Stone, NH-19, Delhi-Mathura Road, P.O. Chaumuhan, Mathura, Uttar Pradesh. Students and visitors can use the official university website to explore information about academics, admissions, campus facilities and other university services.

Academic Programs

GLA University offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma and doctoral programs. Undergraduate options include B.Tech programs in Computer Science and Engineering, Electronics and Communication Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering and Biotechnology. The university also offers programs such as BCA, BBA, B.Pharm, B.Com, B.A., B.Sc., BA LLB and BBA LLB.

Postgraduate programs include M.Tech, MBA, MCA, M.Pharm, M.Sc., M.Com and other specialized programs. The university also offers doctoral programs in areas such as Computer Science and Engineering, Electronics and Communication Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Pharmacy, Management and other disciplines. Students should always verify the latest course details and eligibility criteria on the official university website.

Students interested in computer science can explore B.Tech CSE, B.Tech CSE with specializations, BCA and other technology-oriented programs. The official course page provides the current list of available programs and should be checked before applying.

Campus Facilities

GLA University provides facilities intended to support classroom education, practical training and student development. The campus includes laboratories, classrooms, libraries, hostels, sports facilities, cafeterias and other areas used by students during their academic life.

Laboratories provide students with opportunities to apply concepts learned in classrooms through practical activities. Admission Information

GLA University provides admission opportunities for undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma and doctoral programs. Admission requirements depend on the selected course, so students should carefully check the eligibility criteria before beginning the application process.
